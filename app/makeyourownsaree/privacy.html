Privacy policy

Make Your Own Saree (Android app, package io.github.teatimecrumbles.makeyourownsaree) · Effective 13 November 2026

Summary

Make Your Own Saree does not collect, share or sell any personal or device data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers, and the app has no internet permission.

Who makes the app

Make Your Own Saree is made by Mohammad Motiur Rahman, an independent developer who publishes apps as Teatime Crumbles. Contact: mohammadmotiurrahman@gmail.com

Buying the app

Make Your Own Saree is a paid app sold on Google Play. Google handles the purchase, the payment and refunds under its own terms and the Google Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy). The developer never receives your card or other payment details, and the app itself does not read or store anything about your purchase.

For each sale, Google gives the developer the order details that Google includes in its sales reports, such as the order number, the date, the price and taxes, the device model and the country of purchase (for some countries also the city, state or province and postal code). The developer uses them only for accounting, taxes and questions about an order, does not combine them with any other data, and keeps them only as long as accounting and tax rules require.

If you write to the developer, for example about a refund, your e-mail address and anything you include (such as an order number) are used only to answer you.

Your work in the app

Your colouring, the saree you last opened, the cloth and motif size you chose, the colour theme and the work in each studio are kept only on your phone, in the app’s own storage. They are deleted when you clear the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstall the app.

Android cloud backup is switched off for this app, so your work is not copied to your Google account. If you move to a new phone with Android’s device-to-device transfer, your saved work can be copied directly to the new phone. Nothing is sent to the developer.

Pictures

When you save a picture of your saree, it is stored in the Pictures › Make Your Own Saree folder on your phone (Android 10 and newer). On Android 7 to 9, and whenever you tap Share, the share screen opens instead and you choose where the picture goes; what happens then depends on the app you choose. For the share screen, the app keeps a temporary copy of the picture in its own cache, which other apps cannot read; the copy is deleted the next time you open the app once it is more than a day old. Android may also clear this cache itself, and uninstalling the app deletes it.

Internet

Everything the app needs, including its fonts and the reference photographs, is inside the app. It has no internet permission, so it cannot connect to the internet. The app also tells Android System WebView, which displays the app’s pages, not to send usage statistics about this app. Links to sources, museum pages and image collections open in your web browser, where that website’s own privacy policy applies.

Permissions

The app asks for no permissions: no camera, contacts, location, microphone, phone, storage or internet access.

Children

Make Your Own Saree is not directed at children under 13. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

The app sends nothing to the developer, so the developer holds no data from the app to delete or hand over. Everything the app stores is on your phone and under your control (see “Your work in the app”). The order details described under “Buying the app” are kept only as long as accounting and tax rules require; to ask about them, or to have your e-mails deleted, write to the address below.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date, and the copy inside the app will be updated in the next version of the app. Questions: mohammadmotiurrahman@gmail.com

এই অ্যাপের জন্য অ্যান্ড্রয়েডের ক্লাউড ব্যাকআপ বন্ধ, তাই আপনার কাজ আপনার গুগল অ্যাকাউন্টে কপি হয় না। অ্যান্ড্রয়েডের ফোন-থেকে-ফোন স্থানান্তর (device-to-device transfer) দিয়ে নতুন ফোনে গেলে রাখা কাজ সরাসরি নতুন ফোনে কপি হতে পারে। ডেভেলপারের কাছে কিছুই যায় না।

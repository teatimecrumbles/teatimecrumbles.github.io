Privacy policy

Bari Banao: Home Planner (Android app, package io.github.teatimecrumbles.baribanao) · Effective 14 November 2026

Summary

Bari Banao does not collect, share or sell any personal or device data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers, and the app has no internet permission.

Who makes the app

Bari Banao is made by Mohammad Motiur Rahman, an independent developer who publishes apps as Teatime Crumbles. Contact: mohammadmotiurrahman@gmail.com

Your designs

Your designs and settings are kept only on your phone, in the app’s own storage. They are deleted when you delete them in the app, when you clear the app’s storage in Android settings, or when you uninstall the app.

Android cloud backup is switched off for this app, so your designs are not copied to your Google account. If you move to a new phone with Android’s device-to-device transfer, your saved designs can be copied directly to the new phone. Nothing is sent to the developer.

Describing a house in your own words

When you describe a house in your own words (in Bangla or English), the text is read on your phone to fill in the settings. It is not sent anywhere.

Pictures

When you save a picture of your house, it is stored in the Pictures › Bari Banao folder on your phone (Android 10 and newer). On Android 7 to 9 the share screen opens instead and you choose where the picture goes; what happens then depends on the app you choose. For the share screen, the app keeps a temporary copy of the picture in its own cache, which other apps cannot read; the copy is deleted the next time you open the app once it is more than a day old. Android may also clear this cache itself, and uninstalling the app deletes it. Saved pictures stay in Pictures › Bari Banao until you delete them; uninstalling the app does not remove them.

Internet

Everything the app needs is inside the app. It has no internet permission, so it cannot connect to the internet. The app also tells Android System WebView, which displays the app’s pages, not to send usage statistics about this app. Links to sources (for example Banglapedia or Wikipedia pages about a house style) open in your web browser, where that website’s own privacy policy applies.

Permissions

The app asks for no permissions: no camera, contacts, location, microphone, phone, storage or internet access.

Security

Your designs and settings are kept in the app’s private storage, which other apps cannot read. Nothing is sent over the internet; the app has no internet permission. The developer has no server for this app and holds no copy of your designs. Pictures you save to Pictures › Bari Banao or share can be opened by your gallery and by the apps you allow or choose.

Children

Bari Banao is made for adults planning a home and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

The app sends nothing to the developer, so the developer holds no data from the app to delete or hand over. Everything the app stores is on your phone and under your control (see “Your designs”).

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date, and the copy inside the app will be updated in the next version of the app. Questions: mohammadmotiurrahman@gmail.com. If you e-mail the developer, your e-mail address and message are used only to answer you, and are deleted on request.

ছবি

ইন্টারনেট

অ্যাপটির যা কিছু লাগে, সব অ্যাপের ভেতরেই আছে। এর ইন্টারনেটের অনুমতি নেই, তাই এটি ইন্টারনেটে যোগাযোগ করতেই পারে না। অ্যাপের পাতাগুলো যে Android System WebView দেখায়, অ্যাপটি তাকেও এই অ্যাপের ব্যবহারের পরিসংখ্যান পাঠাতে নিষেধ করে। তথ্যসূত্রের লিংক (যেমন কোনো বাড়ির ধরন নিয়ে বাংলাপিডিয়া বা উইকিপিডিয়ার পাতা) আপনার ব্রাউজারে খোলে; সেখানে সেই ওয়েবসাইটের নিজস্ব গোপনীয়তা নীতি প্রযোজ্য।

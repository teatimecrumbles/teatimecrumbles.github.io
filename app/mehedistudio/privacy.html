Mehedi Studio privacy policy

Effective 14 November 2026 · applies to the Mehedi Studio Android app

Mehedi Studio is a henna (mehedi) design app made by Mohammad Motiur Rahman. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: the app sends nothing to the developer, and if you buy Mehedi Studio Pro, Google Play handles the payment.

What the app collects

The app sends nothing to the developer. Mehedi Studio does not send your designs or any personal or sensitive information to the developer or to any server of its own, and it does not sell or share it. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not use the advertising ID. Apart from what you save or share yourself and your own Google backup, the only information that leaves your phone is about your Pro purchase, which Google Play's billing library exchanges with Google Play (see below). Google Play's Data safety section lists this as purchase history: it is used only to unlock Pro and is not shared with anyone else.

Buying Mehedi Studio Pro

Mehedi Studio is free. Some extras (all ready designs, life-size stencil PDFs, the card and message for your artist, and the mehedi night planner) are unlocked by a one-time in-app purchase, Mehedi Studio Pro, sold through Google Play.

  • Google Play takes the payment and keeps the purchase record under your Google account, as described in the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service. The app never sees your card, bank or Google account details, and the developer never sees your card or bank details.
  • To unlock Pro, the app uses Google's Play Billing library, which talks to the Google Play Store app on your phone. It contacts Google Play when the app starts, each time you come back to it, and when you tap Unlock or "Restore purchase". Google Play tells the app whether this Google account owns Pro, the price in your currency, and a purchase token that the app passes back to Google Play to confirm the purchase. The library may also send Google technical information about these requests. The developer has no server and receives none of this.
  • For each sale, Google gives the developer the order details that Google includes in its sales reports, such as the order number, the date, the price and taxes, the device model and the country of purchase (for some countries also the city, state or province and postal code). The developer uses them only for accounting, taxes and questions about an order, does not combine them with any other data, and keeps them only as long as accounting and tax rules require.
  • The app remembers on your phone whether Pro is unlocked (and its price), so it keeps working offline. "Restore purchase" asks Google Play again, for example on a new phone.
  • Refunds are handled by Google Play. If a purchase is refunded, Pro is locked again the next time the app checks with Google Play.

What stays on your phone

  • Your designs, your settings and the language you choose are saved inside the app's own storage on your phone, so you can come back to them. If Android backup is turned on, Android also keeps a copy in your own Google backup (see below).
  • The details you type for your artist (such as your name, the date and time, the place and notes) are saved with the design, also in design files you save, and leave the phone only in a card, message or file that you share, save or copy.
  • "Copy message" puts the message for your artist on the clipboard; the app never reads the clipboard.
  • The night planner suggests a city from your phone's time zone and saves the city you choose on your phone. The app has no location permission.
  • If you choose a photo of your hand to trace over, the app reads it on your phone only. The photo is not copied into your saved designs and is never sent anywhere.
  • To add a photo or open a design file, the app uses Android's photo or file picker and reads only what you pick. It does not ask for permission to see your photos or files.

Files you save or share

When you save a picture, a stencil PDF or a design file, it goes to the Mehedi Studio folder in your phone's Pictures or Downloads, or, on Android 8 and 9, to the place you pick. When you share a design card, Android's share screen sends it to the app and person you choose, such as WhatsApp. For sharing, the app keeps a temporary copy in its private cache; copies older than a day are deleted when you share again. Mehedi Studio never sees what the other app does with it.

Internet

Everything the app needs is built in, and it works offline. Android lists three permissions that need no asking: internet access and network state, which Google Play's billing library uses, and Google Play billing itself. The design screen blocks every outside connection, and it turns off Android WebView's usage reports and Safe Browsing lookups. Links to the sources in the halal and care guide, and the link to this policy, open in your browser only when you tap them.

Security

Your designs, settings and the details for your artist are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps on your phone cannot read. The design screen sends nothing over the internet; the only part of the app that goes online is Google Play's billing library, which talks to Google over encrypted connections. The developer has no server and holds no copy of your designs. Files you save to Pictures or Downloads can be opened by your gallery or files app and by any app you allow; a card you share goes to the app you choose.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your designs stay on your phone until you delete them in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app; files you saved stay where you saved them until you delete them there. The app sends nothing to the developer, so there is no copy of your designs to request or delete from us. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include the app's designs in your own Google backup, under your control. The order details described under "Buying Mehedi Studio Pro" are kept only as long as accounting and tax rules require; to ask about them, or to have your e-mails deleted, write to the address below.

Children

Mehedi Studio is not directed at children under 13, and the app sends the developer no information about anyone, including children. Purchases follow Google Play's own rules for family accounts and purchase approval.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: mohammadmotiurrahman@gmail.com. If you write, your e-mail address and message are used only to answer you, and are deleted when you ask.

আপনার নকশা, সেটিংস আর শিল্পীর জন্য লেখা তথ্য অ্যাপের নিজস্ব স্টোরেজে থাকে, যা ফোনের অন্য অ্যাপ পড়তে পারে না। নকশার পাতা ইন্টারনেটে কিছুই পাঠায় না; অ্যাপের একমাত্র যে অংশ ইন্টারনেটে যায়, তা Google Play-র বিলিং লাইব্রেরি, যা এনক্রিপ্ট করা সংযোগে Google-এর সঙ্গে কথা বলে। ডেভেলপারের কোনো সার্ভার নেই, আর আপনার নকশার কোনো কপিও তাঁর কাছে নেই। Pictures বা Downloads-এ সেভ করা ফাইল আপনার গ্যালারি বা ফাইল অ্যাপ আর যেসব অ্যাপকে আপনি অনুমতি দেন, সেগুলো খুলতে পারে; শেয়ার করা কার্ড যায় আপনার বেছে নেওয়া অ্যাপে।

তথ্য রাখা ও মুছে ফেলা

অ্যাপে মুছে ফেললে, অ্যাপের স্টোরেজ মুছলে বা অ্যাপ আনইনস্টল করলে নকশাগুলো ফোন থেকে মুছে যায়; সেভ করা ফাইল যেখানে রেখেছেন সেখানেই থাকে, যতক্ষণ না আপনি সেখানে মুছে ফেলেন। অ্যাপ ডেভেলপারের কাছে কিছুই পাঠায় না, তাই ডেভেলপারের কাছে আপনার নকশার এমন কোনো কপি নেই, যা চাইতে বা মুছতে বলতে হবে। অ্যান্ড্রয়েড ব্যাকআপ চালু থাকলে অ্যান্ড্রয়েড আপনার নিজের Google ব্যাকআপে নকশার একটি কপি রাখতে পারে, যা আপনার নিয়ন্ত্রণে থাকে। "মেহেদি স্টুডিও প্রো কেনা" অংশে বলা অর্ডারের তথ্য শুধু হিসাব ও করের নিয়মে যতদিন দরকার ততদিন রাখা হয়; এ নিয়ে জানতে, বা আপনার পাঠানো ই-মেইল মুছে ফেলতে, নিচের ঠিকানায় লিখুন।

শিশু

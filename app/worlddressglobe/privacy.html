World Dress Globe · Privacy policy

World Dress Globe (Android app, package io.github.teatimecrumbles.worlddressglobe) · Effective 14 November 2026

In short: your colorings stay on your phone. The app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and we do not collect, sell or share any personal data.

Who makes the app

World Dress Globe (“the app”) is made by Mohammad Motiur Rahman, an independent developer in Bangladesh who publishes apps as Teatime Crumbles (“we”). Questions: mohammadmotiurrahman@gmail.com.

What the app keeps on your phone

The colors you put on each page, your gallery, your passport stamps, your settings (sounds, vibration, color by number), whether you have seen the welcome tour, and Google Play’s last answer about your purchase. They are kept only in the app’s own storage on your phone and are never sent to us.

Pictures you share

When you tap “Share or save”, the app makes a picture of your page, keeps a temporary copy in its own cache and opens Android’s share screen. The picture goes only where you choose, for example your photos, Drive or WhatsApp. Copies older than a day are deleted when the app starts, and Android may clear the cache at any time.

Purchases

The full atlas is a one-time purchase through Google Play. Google handles the payment under its own privacy policy; we never see your card, your payment details, your name or your e-mail address. The app asks Google Play whether the full atlas has been bought on the Google account on your phone, keeps that yes or no on the phone so the atlas also opens offline, and confirms a new purchase to Google Play. If a purchase is refunded, the atlas locks again. Google Play’s billing component inside the app may send Google technical information about purchase requests, for Google’s own running of Google Play billing; we do not receive it.

Ratings

After your third finished page, the app asks Google Play once to show its rating card. Google Play decides whether to show it. A rating or review you write there goes to Google Play, under Google’s terms, and may appear publicly on Google Play; the app does not receive it.

Reporting a mistake

“Report a mistake” copies the report you write, or opens your own e-mail app with it addressed to us. Nothing is sent unless you send it. If you e-mail us, we receive your e-mail address and your message, use them only to answer you and correct the app, and do not share them.

Internet and links

The app’s pages, drawings and stories are inside the app and work without the internet; the pages are blocked from loading anything from the internet or sending anything to it. Buying or restoring the full atlas, and the rating card, use your connection to Google Play. Links you tap, to the sources of the stories and to Wikimedia Commons, open in your browser, and those websites have their own privacy policies. The app also tells Android System WebView, which shows the app’s pages, not to send its usage statistics to Google.

Permissions

The app does not ask you for any permission. Android lists four that need no asking: internet access and network state, used by Google Play’s billing component; Google Play billing itself; and vibration, for a light tap on each fill, which you can switch off in the app’s menu. The app has no access to your camera, contacts, location, microphone, photos or files, and it uses no advertising ID.

Backups

If Android backup is turned on, your phone may include the app’s data, such as your colorings, in its Google backup. That backup is handled by Google, and we cannot see it.

Security

Your colorings are kept in the app’s private storage, which other apps on the phone cannot read. We hold no copy of them, so nothing of yours is on any server of ours.

Deleting your data

Use “Clear my colorings on this device” in the app’s menu, or clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app, to remove everything. We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to delete or hand over. The full atlas stays with your Google account and comes back with “Restore purchase”.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

কেনাকাটা

পুরো অ্যাটলাস Google Play-র মাধ্যমে একবারের কেনাকাটা। টাকা নেওয়ার কাজ করে Google, তার নিজের গোপনীয়তা নীতি অনুযায়ী; আপনার কার্ড, পেমেন্টের তথ্য, নাম বা ইমেইল আমরা কখনো দেখি না। অ্যাপ শুধু Google Play-কে জিজ্ঞেস করে ফোনের Google অ্যাকাউন্টে পুরো অ্যাটলাস কেনা হয়েছে কি না, সেই হ্যাঁ বা না ফোনে রাখে যাতে ইন্টারনেট ছাড়াও অ্যাটলাস খোলে, আর নতুন কেনাকাটা Google Play-কে নিশ্চিত করে। টাকা ফেরত দেওয়া হলে অ্যাটলাস আবার বন্ধ হয়ে যায়। অ্যাপের ভেতরের Google Play বিলিং অংশ কেনার অনুরোধ নিয়ে কিছু প্রযুক্তিগত তথ্য Google-কে পাঠাতে পারে, Google Play বিলিং চালানোর জন্য; সেটা আমরা পাই না।

রেটিং

আপনার তৃতীয় পাতা শেষ হলে অ্যাপ একবার Google Play-কে তার রেটিং কার্ড দেখাতে বলে। দেখানো হবে কি না, তা ঠিক করে Google Play। সেখানে দেওয়া রেটিং বা রিভিউ যায় Google Play-তে, Google-এর শর্ত অনুযায়ী, আর তা Google Play-তে সবার জন্য দেখা যেতে পারে; অ্যাপ তা পায় না।

“Report a mistake” আপনার লেখা রিপোর্ট কপি করে, অথবা আমাদের ঠিকানায় লেখা অবস্থায় আপনার নিজের ইমেইল অ্যাপ খোলে। আপনি না পাঠালে কিছুই যায় না। আপনি ইমেইল করলে আমরা আপনার ইমেইল ঠিকানা আর বার্তা পাই, শুধু উত্তর দিতে আর অ্যাপ ঠিক করতে ব্যবহার করি, কাউকে দিই না।

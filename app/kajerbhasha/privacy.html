Privacy policy

Kajer Bhasha: Job Phrases (Android app, package io.github.teatimecrumbles.kajerbhasha) · Effective 14 November 2026

Summary

Kajer Bhasha does not collect, share or sell any personal data. Everything stays on your phone. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, trackers or payments, and the app sends nothing over the internet.

Who makes the app

Kajer Bhasha is made by Mohammad Motiur Rahman, an independent developer who publishes apps as Teatime Crumbles. Contact: mohammadmotiurrahman@gmail.com

What is saved on your phone

Your choices (your language, the country and the job), the phrases you saved or marked as known, your practice progress and the text size are saved only on your phone, in the app’s own storage. If a trainer gives you a voice file and you load it, its recordings are kept in the app’s storage too. There is no account and no sign-in. The developer does not receive any of this and cannot see it. “Clear my progress” in More deletes your saved phrases and progress. Everything is deleted when you clear the app’s storage in Android settings (Settings › Apps › Kajer Bhasha › Storage) or uninstall the app.

If your phone backs up its apps to your Google account, Android may include this saved progress, so that it can come back on a new phone. That backup is kept by Google under your Google account settings; the developer cannot see it.

Hearing the phrases

When you tap play, the app gives the phrase’s text (for example “Thank you” or “شكراً”) to your phone’s own text-to-speech engine, usually Google’s Speech Services, which reads it aloud. The app sends it nothing else. That engine is part of your phone, and its own settings and privacy policy apply; it normally works on the phone without the internet.

Emergency numbers and links

Tapping an emergency number opens your phone’s dialler with that number; the call is made only if you make it. Links to this policy open in your web browser, where that website’s own privacy policy applies. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc., which logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes (see the GitHub Privacy Statement).

What the app does not do

No ads, no analytics, no tracking, no crash reports, no account and no in-app purchases. The phrases, the fonts and everything else are inside the app; nothing is downloaded. The app also tells Android System WebView, which displays the app’s pages, not to send usage statistics about this app.

Permissions

Internet only (Android gives it to the app’s web view; the app itself sends nothing). The app does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files. Loading a voice file uses Android’s own file picker, which gives the app only the file you choose.

Children

Kajer Bhasha is made for adults who are going to work abroad and for their trainers. It is not directed at children, and it collects no data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Nothing is sent to the developer, so the developer holds no data about you to delete or hand over. Everything the app stores is on your phone and under your control (see “What is saved on your phone”).

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: mohammadmotiurrahman@gmail.com

Sporsho Chitro – Privacy Policy

App: Sporsho Chitro (স্পর্শ চিত্র), package com.sporshochitro.app
Developer: Mohammad Motiur Rahman (teatimecrumbles)
Contact: mohammadmotiurrahman@gmail.com
Effective date: 14 November 2026

Summary

Sporsho Chitro does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal or device data. The app works entirely offline on your device. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

1. Photos and camera

You can take a photo of a diagram with your phone's camera app or choose an image from your gallery using the Android photo picker. The app does not request the camera or storage permission; you choose each photo yourself. The image is processed only on your device to create tactile lines. It is never uploaded or sent anywhere. A photo taken with the camera is kept briefly in the app's private cache and deleted the next time the app starts.

2. Labels and files you create

Text you type (titles and labels) and the files you export (PDF, SVG, BRF, PEF, ZIP) stay on your device. Files are saved only to the location you pick in the Android "Save" dialog, and they stay there until you delete them. The developer cannot see them.

3. Settings stored on your device

The app remembers your preferences, such as language, paper size, orientation and line thickness, in the app's private storage on your phone. This data never leaves your device and is not backed up by the app. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

4. Network

All of the app's content, including its fonts, is bundled inside the app. The app declares Android's INTERNET permission for its built-in WebView, but it makes no network requests and blocks any attempt to load content from the internet. The "Full privacy policy" link in the app opens this page in your browser; the browser loads it, not the app.

5. Third parties

The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or social-media SDKs. Google Play may collect information under Google's own privacy policy when you download the app. That is outside this app's control.

6. Children

The app is designed for teachers, parents and braille transcribers. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Your rights and data deletion

The app sends nothing to the developer, so the developer holds nothing from the app to access, correct or delete. You can remove all locally stored settings at any time: Settings → Apps → Sporsho Chitro → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the app.

8. Security

Your settings, and a camera photo while it is in the app's cache, are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. Nothing is sent over the internet. The developer has no server for this app and holds no copy of your photos, labels or files. The app cannot read any of your other files. Files you save through the Android "Save" dialog can be opened by the apps you allow, such as your file manager or the cloud drive you saved them to.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. If a future version ever collects data, it will ask for your consent first and the Google Play "Data safety" section will be updated.

10. Contact

Questions: mohammadmotiurrahman@gmail.com. If you e-mail the developer, your e-mail address and message are used only to answer you, and are deleted on request.

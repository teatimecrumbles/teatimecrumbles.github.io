Privacy policy

Ek Mutho Moyda (Android app, package io.github.teatimecrumbles.ekmuthomoyda) · Effective 14 November 2026

Summary

Ek Mutho Moyda does not collect, share or sell any personal or device data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics, trackers or purchases. The app is free and works without the internet.

Who makes the app

Ek Mutho Moyda is made by Mohammad Motiur Rahman, an independent developer who publishes apps as Teatime Crumbles. Contact: mohammadmotiurrahman@gmail.com

What the app does

Ek Mutho Moyda is a baking book: recipes for cakes, biscuits, pastry and bread, each with a short animation drawn on the phone, and a tour of bakeries around the world. You do not have to sign in or give any information to use it.

What is stored on your phone

The app remembers one setting on the phone itself, in the app’s own storage: whether you chose the light or the dark look. It is never sent anywhere. It is deleted when you clear the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstall the app.

Android cloud backup is switched off for this app, so nothing is copied to a Google account. If you move to a new phone with Android’s device-to-device transfer, the setting can be copied directly to the new phone. Nothing is sent to the developer.

Internet

Everything the app needs, including its recipes, animations, map and fonts, is inside the app. The app does not have the Android permission to use the internet, and it blocks every request that is not for one of its own files. The app also tells Android System WebView, which displays the app’s pages, not to send usage statistics about this app.

Links to other websites

The Sources list in the app links to the articles the recipe histories were checked against (for example Wikipedia or newspapers). When you tap one, it opens in your own web browser, outside the app; that website’s own privacy policy applies there. The Natural Earth link on the About page opens the same way. Nothing is opened unless you tap a link.

Permissions

The app asks for no permissions: no internet, camera, contacts, location, microphone, phone or storage access.

Children

The app is not designed specially for children, but it collects nothing from anyone, children included. It has no ads, no sign-in, no chat, no purchases and no third-party code that collects data. The developer does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Security

The one setting the app keeps stays in its private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read. The developer has no server and holds no copy.

Deleting your data

The app sends nothing to the developer, so the developer holds no data about you, except an e-mail you choose to send (for example with a question or a correction): your address and message are used only to answer you and are deleted on request. The one setting the app keeps is on your phone and under your control (see “What is stored on your phone”).

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date, and the copy inside the app will be updated in the next version of the app. Questions: mohammadmotiurrahman@gmail.com

Privacy policy

PDF Fit (Android app) by Mohammad Motiur Rahman · Effective 13 November 2026

Summary

PDF Fit does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers, and the app has no internet permission.

Your PDFs and photos

The PDFs and photos you add (for example certificates, passport pages, ID cards or mark sheets) are opened, joined and compressed on your phone. They are never uploaded to a server, and the developer never sees them. The app keeps them in memory only while it is open; it does not keep copies of them after you close it.

Files you choose

When you press “Add PDF or photos”, Android’s file picker opens. The app can read only the files you pick there, nothing else on your phone.

Camera

The app has no camera permission. When you press “Take photo”, your phone’s camera app opens and takes the picture. It hands the photo to PDF Fit as a temporary file in the app’s own storage, which other apps cannot read. The file is deleted when you leave the app with the Back button; if the app was closed in another way, it is deleted the next time you open the app. Uninstalling the app deletes it as well.

Saved PDFs

A PDF you save is written to the Downloads › PDFFit folder on your phone (on Android 9 and older, to the place you choose). It stays there until you delete it. Uploading it to a portal is something you do yourself, in your browser or another app.

Settings

The app remembers the size limit, page size and colour you chose last, only on your phone, in the app’s own storage. Android backup is switched off for this app, so these settings are not copied to your Google account or to another phone. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Internet and links

Everything the app needs is inside the app. It has no internet permission, so it cannot connect to the internet at all. Links to portals’ own notices and to licences open in your browser, which follows its own privacy terms.

Purchase

PDF Fit is a paid app sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment under Google’s own privacy policy; the app never sees your payment details. Google gives the developer order records (such as an order number, the date, the price and the country) for accounting, taxes and refunds. They are used only for those purposes and are not sold or shared.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from users of any age, including children.

Privacy policy

PDF Fit (Android app) by Mohammad Motiur Rahman · Effective 14 November 2026

Summary

PDF Fit sends nothing to the developer or to anyone else. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers, and the app has no internet permission. Because the app is sold on Google Play, Google gives the developer the order details described under “Purchase”.

Your PDFs and photos

The PDFs and photos you add (for example certificates, passport pages, ID cards or mark sheets) are opened, joined and compressed on your phone. They are never uploaded to a server, and the developer never sees them. The app keeps them in memory only while it is open and does not keep copies of them after you close it (for a photo taken with the camera, see “Camera”).

Files you choose

When you press “Add PDF or photos”, Android’s file picker opens. The app can read only the files you pick there, nothing else on your phone.

Camera

The app has no camera permission. When you press “Take photo”, your phone’s camera app opens and takes the picture. It hands the photo to PDF Fit as a temporary file in the app’s own storage, which other apps cannot read. The file is deleted when you leave the app with the Back button; if the app was closed in another way, it is deleted the next time you open the app. Uninstalling the app deletes it as well.

Saved PDFs

A PDF you save is written to the Downloads › PDFFit folder on your phone (on Android 9 and older, to the place you choose). It stays there until you delete it. Uploading it to a portal is something you do yourself, in your browser or another app.

Settings

The app remembers the size limit you chose last, only on your phone, in the app’s own storage. Android backup is switched off for this app, so this setting is not copied to your Google account or to another phone. Clearing the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app removes it.

Internet and links

Everything the app needs is inside the app. It has no internet permission, so it cannot connect to the internet at all. Links to portals’ own notices, and the links to the libraries’ websites on the licences page, open in your browser, which follows its own privacy terms.

Security

The size limit and any camera photo are kept in the app’s private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read. Nothing is sent over the internet: the app has no internet permission. The developer has no server for the app and holds no copy of your files or settings. A PDF you save to Downloads › PDFFit (or to the place you choose) can be opened by other apps that you allow to read your files, such as a file manager or the browser you upload it with.

Purchase

PDF Fit is a paid app sold through Google Play. Google handles the purchase, the payment and refunds under Google’s own privacy policy. The app and the developer never see your card or other payment details, and the app itself does not read or store anything about your purchase.

For each sale, Google gives the developer the order details that Google includes in its sales reports, such as the order number, the date, the price and taxes, the device model and the country of purchase (for some countries also the city, state or province and postal code). The developer uses them only for accounting, taxes and questions about an order, does not combine them with any other data, and keeps them only as long as accounting and tax rules require. To ask about them, write to the address below.

Children

The app is not directed at children. It sends nothing to the developer about anyone, including children.

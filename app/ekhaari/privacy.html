Privacy policy

Ek Haari (Android app, package io.github.teatimecrumbles.ekhaari) · Effective 14 November 2026

Summary

Ek Haari does not collect, share or sell any personal or device data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers, and the app has no internet permission.

Who makes the app

Ek Haari is made by Mohammad Motiur Rahman, an independent developer who publishes apps as Teatime Crumbles. Contact: mohammadmotiurrahman@gmail.com

What the app does

Ek Haari is a book of rice dishes from around the world, each with a short animated how-to, and a world tour on a map. All recipes, animations and the map are inside the app. You do not type in, upload or record anything.

What is kept on your device

The only thing the app remembers is your choice of light or dark theme, if you change it. It is kept on your device, in the app’s own storage, and is deleted when you clear the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstall the app.

Android cloud backup is switched off for this app, so nothing is copied to your Google account. If you move to a new phone with Android’s device-to-device transfer, the theme choice can be copied directly to the new phone. Nothing is sent to the developer.

Internet

Everything the app needs, including its fonts, is inside the app. It has no internet permission, so it cannot connect to the internet. The app also tells Android System WebView, which displays the app’s pages, not to send usage statistics about this app. Each recipe lists its sources as web links; when you tap one, it opens in your web browser, where that website’s own privacy policy applies.

Permissions

The app asks for no permissions: no camera, contacts, location, microphone, phone, storage or internet access.

Children

Ek Haari is made for adults who cook and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Nothing is sent to the developer, so the developer holds no data about you to delete or hand over. The one setting the app stores is on your device and under your control (see “What is kept on your device”).

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date, and the copy inside the app will be updated in the next version of the app. Questions: mohammadmotiurrahman@gmail.com

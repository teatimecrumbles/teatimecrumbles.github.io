Privacy policy

PhotoFit (Android app, package io.github.teatimecrumbles.photofit) · Effective 14 November 2026

Summary

PhotoFit makes passport, visa and ID photos on your phone. Your photos never leave your phone unless you share them yourself. The app does not collect, share or sell any personal or device data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers.

Who makes the app

PhotoFit is made by Mohammad Motiur Rahman, an independent developer who publishes apps as Teatime Crumbles. Contact: mohammadmotiurrahman@gmail.com

Your photos

When you tap “Take a photo”, PhotoFit opens your phone’s camera app, which hands the one picture you take back to PhotoFit. When you tap “Choose a photo”, Android’s photo picker lets you pick one picture; PhotoFit sees only that picture, not the rest of your gallery. The picture is resized, cropped, brightened and given a cleaner background on the phone itself. It is never uploaded, and the developer never sees it.

A photo taken with the camera is written by the camera app into the app’s private temporary folder, which other apps cannot read. It is deleted when you take the next photo or close the app, or at the latest the next time the app starts. PhotoFit keeps no other copy: the photo you are working on stays only in the app’s memory until you choose another photo or close the app, unless you save it.

Finding the face (Auto-fit)

“Auto-fit” uses the face finder built into Android, which works offline on the phone, to find where the eyes are, so the head can be sized and centred for the chosen document. It does not recognise or identify anyone, nothing about the face is stored, and nothing is sent anywhere. The eye position is kept only in the app’s memory while that photo is open, to place it and check the head size, and is never saved.

Saving and sharing

“Save” puts the finished photo or print sheet in your phone’s gallery, in Pictures › PhotoFit (on Android 7 to 9 you choose the place yourself). “Share” hands the finished picture to the app you choose, for example an e-mail or messaging app or a print shop’s app; from then on, that app’s own privacy policy applies. A copy made for sharing is kept in the app’s private temporary folder, which only the app you chose can read, and is deleted the next time you share or close the app, or at the latest the next time the app starts.

Settings

The app remembers your language, the last photo size, the resolution and a custom size, only on the phone, in the app’s own storage. They are deleted when you clear the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Android cloud backup and phone-to-phone transfer are switched off for this app.

PhotoFit Pro (in-app purchase) and your country

PhotoFit is free to download, and choosing, fitting and checking photos is free everywhere. Saving, sharing and print sheets are part of PhotoFit Pro, a single one-time purchase through Google Play (US$1.99 or the local equivalent); there is no subscription. In Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, the Maldives, Nepal, Pakistan and Sri Lanka, PhotoFit Pro is free and nothing is sold.

To know which applies, the app asks Google Play for the country of your Google Play account. Only when Google Play cannot answer does it use the last answer it got, or the country of the SIM card. The country is kept on the phone and never sent to the developer.

The payment is handled entirely by Google Play, under Google’s privacy policy. The app and the developer never see your card or payment details. The app asks Google Play whether PhotoFit Pro has been bought and keeps that yes or no on the phone; it sends nothing about the purchase to the developer. Google Play’s billing component, which is part of the app, connects to Google Play each time you open the app or come back to it, to check the country, the price and whether PhotoFit Pro has been bought, and when you buy PhotoFit Pro or restore the purchase; it may send Google technical information about these requests, under Google’s privacy policy.

For each sale of PhotoFit Pro, Google gives the developer the order details that Google includes in its sales reports, such as the order number, the date, the price and taxes, the device model and the country of purchase (for some countries also the city, state or province and postal code). The developer uses them only for accounting, taxes and questions about an order, and keeps them only as long as accounting and tax rules require.

If you write to the developer, for example about a purchase or a refund, your e-mail address and anything you include (such as an order number) are used only to answer you, and are deleted on request.

Internet

Everything the app needs is inside the app, and it works without the internet. The app’s pages cannot connect to the internet at all: the app blocks every request that is not for one of its own files. The only part that uses the network is Google Play’s billing component, to talk to Google Play about PhotoFit Pro and the country. The app also tells Android System WebView, which displays the app’s pages, not to send usage statistics about this app.

Permissions

The app never asks you for a permission: no camera, contacts, location, microphone, phone or storage access (the camera app and the photo picker do not need one). Google Play’s billing component includes three permissions that Android grants without asking: to use Google Play billing, to use the network and to check whether the phone is online. They are used only for PhotoFit Pro.

Children

PhotoFit is a tool for adults and families preparing documents; it is not made for children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Security

Your photo, its temporary copies and your settings stay in the app’s memory and private storage, which other apps cannot read; the camera app and the app you share with get access only to the one file they need. The developer has no server and holds no copy of your photos. Photos you save go to your gallery, where apps that you allow to see your photos, for example a backup app, can open them (on Android 7 to 9 they go to the place you chose).

Deleting your data

The app sends nothing to the developer, so there is nothing from the app for the developer to delete or hand over. Photos you saved are in your gallery, under your control. Your purchase of PhotoFit Pro belongs to your Google account; Google Play keeps its record, and the app checks it again on a new phone. The order details described under “PhotoFit Pro (in-app purchase) and your country” are kept only as long as accounting and tax rules require; to ask about them, or to have your e-mails deleted, write to mohammadmotiurrahman@gmail.com.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date, and the copy inside the app will be updated in the next version of the app. Questions: mohammadmotiurrahman@gmail.com

Política de privacidad

PhotoFit (app para Android) · Desarrollador: Mohammad Motiur Rahman (Teatime Crumbles) · En vigor desde el 14 de noviembre de 2026

PhotoFit hace fotos de pasaporte, visado y documento de identidad en tu teléfono. Tus fotos nunca salen del teléfono salvo que las compartas tú. La app no recoge, comparte ni vende datos personales ni del dispositivo. Sin cuentas, anuncios, analítica ni rastreadores.

La foto llega desde la app de cámara o el selector de fotos de Android; el recorte, el tamaño, la luz y el fondo se hacen en el teléfono y no se sube nada. «Ajuste automático» usa el detector de caras de Android, sin conexión, solo para encontrar los ojos; no reconoce a nadie ni guarda ni envía nada.

Guardar, compartir y las hojas para imprimir forman parte de PhotoFit Pro, una compra única en Google Play (1,99 US$ o su equivalente local). Es gratis en Bangladés, Bután, la India, Maldivas, Nepal, Pakistán y Sri Lanka. Para saberlo, la app pregunta a Google Play el país de tu cuenta (o usa la última respuesta o el país de la SIM); el país se queda en el teléfono. Google Play gestiona todo el pago. Por cada venta, Google da al desarrollador los datos del pedido que incluye en sus informes de ventas (como el número de pedido, la fecha, el precio y los impuestos, el modelo del dispositivo y el país; en algunos países también la ciudad, la provincia y el código postal); el desarrollador los usa solo para la contabilidad, los impuestos y las consultas sobre un pedido, y los guarda solo el tiempo que exigen las normas contables y fiscales. La app no pide ningún permiso. Preguntas: mohammadmotiurrahman@gmail.com (tu dirección y tu mensaje se usan solo para responderte y se borran si lo pides). El texto completo y válido es el inglés.

Politique de confidentialité

PhotoFit (application Android) · Développeur : Mohammad Motiur Rahman (Teatime Crumbles) · En vigueur depuis le 14 novembre 2026

PhotoFit crée des photos de passeport, de visa et d’identité sur votre téléphone. Vos photos ne quittent jamais le téléphone, sauf si vous les partagez vous-même. L’application ne collecte, ne partage ni ne vend aucune donnée personnelle ou de l’appareil. Ni compte, ni publicité, ni statistiques, ni traceur.

La photo vient de l’appareil photo ou du sélecteur de photos d’Android ; recadrage, format, lumière et fond se font sur le téléphone, rien n’est envoyé. « Cadrage auto » utilise le détecteur de visage intégré d’Android, hors ligne, uniquement pour situer les yeux ; il ne reconnaît personne et ne garde ni n’envoie rien.

Enregistrer, partager et les planches à imprimer font partie de PhotoFit Pro, un achat unique sur Google Play (1,99 $ US ou l’équivalent local). Il est gratuit au Bangladesh, au Bhoutan, en Inde, aux Maldives, au Népal, au Pakistan et au Sri Lanka. Pour le savoir, l’application demande à Google Play le pays de votre compte (ou utilise la dernière réponse ou le pays de la carte SIM) ; le pays reste sur le téléphone. Google Play gère tout le paiement. Pour chaque vente, Google transmet au développeur les détails de commande figurant dans ses rapports de ventes (par exemple le numéro de commande, la date, le prix et les taxes, le modèle de l’appareil et le pays ; pour certains pays aussi la ville, la région et le code postal) ; le développeur ne les utilise que pour la comptabilité, les impôts et les questions sur une commande, et ne les conserve que le temps exigé par les règles comptables et fiscales. L’application ne demande aucune autorisation. Questions : mohammadmotiurrahman@gmail.com (votre adresse et votre message ne servent qu’à vous répondre et sont supprimés sur demande). Le texte complet faisant foi est en anglais.

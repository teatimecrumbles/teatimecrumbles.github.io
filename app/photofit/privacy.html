Privacy policy

PhotoFit (Android app, package io.github.teatimecrumbles.photofit) · Effective 14 November 2026

Summary

PhotoFit makes passport, visa and ID photos on your phone. Your photos never leave your phone unless you share them yourself. The app does not collect, share or sell any personal or device data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or trackers.

Who makes the app

PhotoFit is made by Mohammad Motiur Rahman, an independent developer who publishes apps as Teatime Crumbles. Contact: mohammadmotiurrahman@gmail.com

Your photos

When you tap “Take a photo”, PhotoFit opens your phone’s camera app, which hands the one picture you take back to PhotoFit. When you tap “Choose a photo”, Android’s photo picker lets you pick one picture; PhotoFit sees only that picture, not the rest of your gallery. The picture is resized, cropped, brightened and given a cleaner background on the phone itself. It is never uploaded, and the developer never sees it.

A photo taken with the camera is kept in the app’s private temporary folder only until it has been opened, and that folder is emptied when the app closes. PhotoFit does not keep your photos: when you leave the photo or close the app, it is gone, unless you saved it.

Finding the face (Auto-fit)

“Auto-fit” uses the face finder built into Android, which works offline on the phone, to find where the eyes are, so the head can be sized and centred for the chosen document. It does not recognise or identify anyone, nothing about the face is stored, and nothing is sent anywhere. The result is used once to place the photo and then forgotten.

Saving and sharing

“Save” puts the finished photo or print sheet in your phone’s gallery, in Pictures › PhotoFit (on Android 7 to 9 you choose the place yourself). “Share” hands the finished picture to the app you choose, for example an e-mail or messaging app or a print shop’s app; from then on, that app’s own privacy policy applies. A copy made for sharing is kept in the app’s private temporary folder and deleted the next time you share and when the app closes.

Settings

The app remembers your language, the last photo size, the resolution and a custom size, only on the phone, in the app’s own storage. They are deleted when you clear the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Android cloud backup and phone-to-phone transfer are switched off for this app.

PhotoFit Pro (in-app purchase) and your country

PhotoFit is free to download, and choosing, fitting and checking photos is free everywhere. Saving, sharing and print sheets are part of PhotoFit Pro, a single one-time purchase through Google Play (US$1.99 or the local equivalent); there is no subscription. In Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, the Maldives, Nepal, Pakistan and Sri Lanka, PhotoFit Pro is free and nothing is sold.

To know which applies, the app asks Google Play for the country of your Google Play account. Only when Google Play cannot answer does it use the last answer it got, or the country of the SIM card. The country is kept on the phone and never sent to the developer.

The payment is handled entirely by Google Play, under Google’s privacy policy. The app and the developer never see your card or payment details. The app asks Google Play whether PhotoFit Pro has been bought and keeps that yes or no on the phone; it sends nothing about the purchase to the developer. Google Play gives the developer only sales reports (such as an order number, the country, the date and the price), which are used only for accounting and refunds. Google Play’s billing component, which is part of the app, connects to Google Play and may send Google technical information about purchase requests, under Google’s privacy policy.

Internet

Everything the app needs is inside the app, and it works without the internet. The app’s pages cannot connect to the internet at all: the app blocks every request that is not for one of its own files. The only part that uses the network is Google Play’s billing component, to talk to Google Play about PhotoFit Pro and the country. The app also tells Android System WebView, which displays the app’s pages, not to send usage statistics about this app.

Permissions

The app never asks you for a permission: no camera, contacts, location, microphone, phone or storage access (the camera app and the photo picker do not need one). Google Play’s billing component includes three permissions that Android grants without asking: to use Google Play billing, to use the network and to check whether the phone is online. They are used only for PhotoFit Pro.

Children

PhotoFit is a tool for adults and families preparing documents; it is not made for children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Nothing is sent to the developer, so the developer holds no data about you to delete or hand over. Photos you saved are in your gallery, under your control. Your purchase of PhotoFit Pro belongs to your Google account; Google Play keeps its record, and the app checks it again on a new phone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date, and the copy inside the app will be updated in the next version of the app. Questions: mohammadmotiurrahman@gmail.com
